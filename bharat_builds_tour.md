# WeMakeDevs x AWS Bharat Builds Tour Hackathon

The **Bharat Builds Tour** is a nationwide hybrid hackathon series organized by **WeMakeDevs** in collaboration with the **AWS Builder Center**. Running from September to December 2026, the tour consists of six separate hackathon stops across India, designed to help university students move beyond tutorials and build practical technology projects using AWS and open-source tools.

## Key Features
*   **Hybrid Format:** Each of the six stops runs as an online hackathon open to students across India, with an additional optional in-person build day held in the host city.
*   **Prizes & Opportunities:** The series features a total prize pool of approximately ₹1 crore. Top-performing students can also earn the opportunity for **fast-track interviews at Amazon** for internships and full-time roles.
*   **Accessibility:** Participation is free. Students can compete solo or in teams of up to 4 members.
*   **Requirements:** Participants must have a WeMakeDevs account and an **AWS Builder Center profile** (with verified student status) to compete and access resources like AWS credits.

## Event Schedule & Stops
*   **Stop 1: First Commit (Sept 17–20, 2026):** The inaugural event of the tour, which included an in-person build day in Bengaluru.
*   **Stop 2: Environmental Hacks (Oct 8–11, 2026):** The current/second stop of the tour, focusing on themes like Air, Heat and Water, and Waste and Energy. It features an optional in-person build day in Delhi (DTU) on October 10.

## How to Participate
1.  **Register:** You only need to register for the tour once to be eligible for all six stops.
2.  **Check-in:** For each specific hackathon stop, you must "check in" when the event opens.
3.  **Build:** You can work on your project online or, if you secure a seat, attend the in-person venue for mentorship, workshops, and networking.

For the latest updates, rules, and to register for upcoming stops, you can visit the official [WeMakeDevs website](https://www.wemakedevs.org).
